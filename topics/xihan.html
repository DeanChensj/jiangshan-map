Early Han combined commanderies with princedoms and used marriage alliances with the Xiongnu. Emperor Wu drove the Xiongnu north, opened the Hexi Corridor and the Silk Road, annexed Nanyue and Gojoseon, and in 60 BCE established the Protectorate of the Western Regions. Wang Mang’s Xin interregnum lasted 8–23 CE. Drag across three stages.
