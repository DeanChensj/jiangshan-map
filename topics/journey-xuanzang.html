玄奘西行求法行迹（627年 — 645年 · 唐贞观元年至贞观十九年）

Xuanzang's Pilgrimage to the Western Regions (627 CE – 645 CE · Tang Dynasty, Zhenguan Era)

玄奘西行求法行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（12 站）

第 1 站 长安（今陕西西安）· 发足长安·孤身西迈 Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Departing Chang'an for the West
627年（贞观元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

为求《瑜伽师地论》梵本真义，年二十八自长安混迹灾民西出秦陇，立誓“不至天竺终不东归”。

Seeking original Sanskrit texts of the Yogacarabhumi-sastra, Xuanzang departed Chang’an alone, vowing never to turn back before India.

《大慈恩寺三藏法师传》：“遂发足西行，自尔孑然孤游。”Biography of the Tripitaka Master: "Thus he set foot westward, traveling onward in solitary resolve."
第 2 站 凉州（今甘肃武威）· 凉州开讲·昼伏夜行 Liangzhou (Wuwei, Gansu) — Preaching in Liangzhou & Night Crossing
627年（贞观元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

于河西都会凉州开讲经论月余，名震葱岭以东；为避边关禁令，由慧威法师遣徒护送昼伏夜行。

After lecturing for a month in Liangzhou to great acclaim, he evaded border bans by traveling at night and hiding by day toward Guazhou.

《大慈恩寺三藏法师传》：“凉州为河西都会，僧徒既多，请留开讲。”Biography of the Tripitaka Master: "Liangzhou was the metropolis of Hexi; its many monks entreated him to stay and lecture."
第 3 站 瓜州（今甘肃瓜州）· 夜渡葫芦河·闯莫贺延碛 Guazhou (Guazhou, Gansu) — Crossing the Gobi of Moheyan
627年（贞观元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

州吏李昌感其诚暗撕牒文；玄奘夜渡葫芦河独闯五烽，于八百里莫贺延碛绝水五日，九死一生抵伊吾。

Crossing the Hulu River past five watchtowers, he survived five days without water in the 800-li Moheyan Desert before reaching Hami.

《大慈恩寺三藏法师传》：“莫贺延碛长八百余里，古曰沙河，上无飞鸟，下无走兽。”Biography of the Tripitaka Master: "The Moheyan Desert stretches over 800 li—no birds above, no beasts below."
第 4 站 高昌王城（今新疆吐鲁番）· 高昌结义·修书通西域 Gaochang (Turpan, Xinjiang) — Sworn Brotherhood with the King of Gaochang
628年（贞观二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

绝食三日明西求大法之志，高昌王麴文泰感泣结为兄弟，修二十四封国书、备资粮护送西行。

Moved by Xuanzang’s three-day fast, King Qu Wentai of Gaochang swore brotherhood and provided 24 diplomatic letters for his journey west.

《大慈恩寺三藏法师传》：“任师西迈，乞垂早食……遂共结为兄弟。”Biography of the Tripitaka Master: "The King pledged to support his westward journey and swore brotherhood with him."
第 5 站 龟兹（今新疆库车）· 驻锡龟兹·翻越凌山雪岭 Kucha (Kuqa, Xinjiang) — Kucha Oasis & Crossing the Icy Tian Shan
628年（贞观二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

在龟兹与木叉毱多论辩经义，留六十余日后翻越积雪千尺的凌山（天山木扎尔特达坂）出天山北麓。

Delayed two months in Kucha by winter snows while debating scriptures, Xuanzang then scaled the glacial Muzart Pass of the Tian Shan.

《大唐西域记》：“山谷积雪，春夏合冻，虽时消泮，寻复结冰。”Great Tang Records on the Western Regions: "The mountain valleys are piled with snow, frozen through spring and summer."
第 6 站 碎叶城（今吉尔吉斯斯坦托克马克）· 过大清池·会西突厥叶护可汗 Suyab (Tokmok, Kyrgyzstan) — Audience with the Western Turkic Khagan at Suyab
628年（贞观二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

循大清池（伊塞克湖）至碎叶城会西突厥统叶护可汗，可汗设宴礼敬并派译官率骑护送南下。

Passing Lake Issyk-Kul to Suyab, he met Tong Yabghu Khagan of the Western Turks, who assigned a multilingual envoy to escort him south.

《大唐西域记》：“清池西北行五百余里至素叶水城，逢突厥叶护可汗方事畋游。”Great Tang Records: "Traveling 500 li northwest from the Clear Lake to Suyab, he met the Turkic Yabghu Khagan on a hunt."
第 7 站 缚喝（今阿富汗巴尔赫）· 过铁门关·参礼小王舍城 Balkh (Balkh, Afghanistan) — Through the Iron Gate to Balkh
628年（贞观二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

经塔什干、撒马尔罕穿铁门关，渡阿姆河抵号称“小王舍城”的缚喝国，与般若羯罗切磋毗婆沙论。

Passing Samarkand and the Iron Gate Pass across the Oxus to Balkh (“Little Rajagrha”), he studied Abhidharma texts at Nava Vihara.

《大唐西域记》：“缚喝国，伽蓝百有余所，僧徒三千余人。”Great Tang Records: "The Kingdom of Balkh has over a hundred monasteries and more than three thousand monks."
第 8 站 健驮逻（今巴基斯坦白沙瓦）· 越雪山礼大佛·入天竺求法 Gandhara (Peshawar, Pakistan) — Crossing the Hindu Kush into Gandhara & India
629年（贞观三年） · 在地图定位此站 →

翻越兴都库什大雪山、礼巴米扬石佛入北天竺健驮逻，后驻迦湿弥罗研经两年，深游五印十余载。

Crossing the Hindu Kush past Bamiyan into Gandhara, he studied two years in Kashmir before journeying across India for over a decade.

《大唐西域记》：“自古大圣贤，多生此国，作论诸师，亦多出此。”Great Tang Records: "Since antiquity, great sages and treatise-masters have arisen in this land of Gandhara."
第 9 站 那烂陀寺（今印度比哈尔邦那烂陀）· 那烂陀寺·师从戒贤学《瑜伽》 Nalanda Monastery (Nalanda, Bihar, India) — Studying the Yogacara under Silabhadra at Nalanda
631年（贞观五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

抵摩揭陀国那烂陀寺，礼百岁住持戒贤论师为师，潜心研习《瑜伽师地论》及诸部经论凡五年。

At Nalanda Monastery in Magadha, he studied the Yogacarabhumi-sastra and Buddhist canons for five years under aged abbot Silabhadra.

《大唐西域记》：“僧徒数千，并俊才高学也，德重当时，声驰异域者，数百余矣。”Great Tang Records: "Its monks number several thousand, all of outstanding talent and learning; several hundred are honored in their time and renowned in foreign lands."
第 10 站 曲女城（今印度北方邦卡瑙季）· 曲女城大会·十八日无人能难 Kanyakubja (Kannauj, Uttar Pradesh, India) — The Grand Debate at Kannauj
642年（贞观十六年） · 在地图定位此站 →

戒日王于曲女城设十八国无遮大会，玄奘任论主悬论门外，十八日无一人能难，获尊“大乘天”。

At King Harsha’s Eighteen-Kingdom Assembly in Kannauj, Xuanzang presided as master of debate; for 18 days no one could refute his thesis.

《大慈恩寺三藏法师传》：“若其间有一字无理能难破者，请斩首相谢。”Biography of the Tripitaka Master: "Should anyone find a single word herein unreasoned and refute it, I offer my head in apology."
第 11 站 于阗（今新疆和田）· 越葱岭载经东归·奉表唐廷 Khotan (Hotan, Xinjiang) — Return via Khotan & Memorial to Emperor Taizong
644年（贞观十八年） · 在地图定位此站 →

载梵本经论越葱岭东归至于阗，遣使向唐太宗奉表陈情；太宗降敕欣慰迎归，命沿途州县护送。

Returning across the Pamirs to Khotan with Sanskrit scriptures, he sent a memorial to Emperor Taizong, who issued an edict welcoming him home.

《进西域记表》：“冒越宪章，私往天竺……历览周游，一十七载。”Memorial to the Throne: "Having ventured beyond the border laws to India, I traveled and observed for seventeen years."
第 12 站 长安（今陕西西安）· 归至京师·开场译经撰记 Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Triumphal Return to Chang'an & Translation Bureau
645年（贞观十九年） · 在地图定位此站 →

返抵长安迎奉六百五十七部梵经，后于弘福寺、大慈恩寺主持译场十九载，撰《大唐西域记》。

Returning to Chang’an with 657 Sanskrit texts, he led the imperial translation bureau for 19 years and compiled Great Tang Records.

《旧唐书·玄奘传》：“贞观十九年，归至京师，太宗见之大悦，与之谈论。”Old Book of Tang: "In the 19th year of Zhenguan he returned to the capital; Emperor Taizong met him with great joy."
