第 2 事 东京司天监Astronomical Bureau, Kaifeng

Shen Kuo heads the Kaifeng Astronomical Bureau, reforming instruments and surveying Bian Canal

沈括奉使契丹抵辽上京据理折冲，沿途聚米山川绘《使契丹图抄》

Envoy Shen Kuo negotiates border disputes at the Liao Supreme Capital and compiles raised-relief maps

第 4 事 延州（延安）Yanzhou (Yan'an)
1081年 · 北宋神宗·元丰四年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

鄜延路经略使沈括镇守延州抗夏，首记陕北“石油”并命名传世

Commanding Yanzhou against Western Xia, Shen Kuo coins the Chinese term shiyou for petroleum

Polymath Shen Kuo retires to Mengxi Garden in Zhenjiang to complete his scientific classic Dream Pool Essays
