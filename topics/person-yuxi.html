传国玉玺（前283—936）· 流传时空图志

Heirloom Seal of the Realm (283 BCE – 936 CE · Qin)

主要时期：秦 时间跨度：前283—936 关联节点：共 13 站 主要足迹：邯郸 · 咸阳 · 轵道 · 长安未央宫

秦始皇命李斯磨楚和氏璧所制，历两汉、魏晋、南朝、隋唐千余载，936年于洛阳玄武楼失传。

Carved under Qin Shi Huang from the Heshi Bi jade, it passed from Qin and Han through Sui and Tang until vanishing at Xuanwu Tower in 936 CE.

一、「传国玉玺」历代流转时空节点（13 站）

以下事件按时间顺序由主图时空编年引擎聚合生成，点击单条右上角可直接在历史地图定位至对应年份与城邑：

第 1 站 邯郸（今河北邯郸）Handan (Handan, Hebei)
前283年 · 周赧王三十二年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

完璧归赵·和氏璧入世：赵惠文王得楚和氏璧，蔺相如奉璧入秦完璧归赵；前228年秦破邯郸灭赵，璧入于秦。

Returning the Jade Intact to Zhao: King Huiwen of Zhao obtained the Heshi Bi jade; Lin Xiangru returned it intact from Qin to Handan until Qin conquered Zhao in 228 BCE.

《资治通鉴》：“乃使其从者衣褐，怀其璧，从径道亡，归璧于赵。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Lin Xiangru had his attendant dress in coarse cloth, conceal the jade, and slip back to Zhao by side roads.'
第 2 站 咸阳（今陕西咸阳）Xianyang (Xianyang, Shaanxi)
前221年 · 秦始皇二十六年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

始皇制玺·受命于天：秦始皇并六国称帝，命李斯磨和氏璧作皇帝玉玺，篆文“受命于天，既寿永昌”，定为正统信物。

Qin Shi Huang Carves the Imperial Seal: Unifying the Six States in 221 BCE, Qin Shi Huang had Li Si carve the Heshi Bi into the Imperial Seal inscribed with eight characters of Heaven’s Mandate.

《资治通鉴》胡注引崔浩：“李斯磨和氏璧作之，文曰‘受命于天，既寿永昌’。”Zizhi Tongjian (Cui Hao note): 'Li Si carved it from the Heshi Bi; the Han emperors wore it through the generations as the Heirloom Seal.'
第 3 站 轵道（今陕西西安灞桥东北）Zhidao (Bashang) (Baqiao, Xi'an)
前206年 · 汉高帝元年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

子婴奉玺·降于轵道：刘邦破峣关至霸上，秦王子婴素车白马封皇帝玺符节降于轵道旁；汉高祖佩之，称“汉传国玺”。

Ziying Surrenders the Seal at Zhidao: In 206 BCE, Qin ruler Ziying rode a white-horse cart to Zhidao near Bashang and surrendered the Imperial Seal to Liu Bang, founding Emperor of Han.

《资治通鉴》：“秦王子婴素车白马，系颈以组，封皇帝玺、符、节，降轵道旁。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'King Ziying of Qin, in a plain cart drawn by a white horse, sealed the Imperial Seal and tallies and surrendered beside Zhidao.'
第 4 站 长安未央宫（今陕西西安未央区）Chang'an (Weiyang Palace) (Weiyang, Xi'an)
8年 · 西汉孺子婴·初始元年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

王莽逼宫·投玺崩角：王莽篡汉遣王舜入未央宫索玺，太皇太后王政君怒投传国玺于地，螭一角微崩，后以金补之。

Empress Dowager Wang Hurls the Seal, Chipping a Horn: Usurping the Han throne in 8 CE, Wang Mang demanded the seal from Grand Empress Dowager Wang, who hurled it down and chipped a dragon horn.

《资治通鉴》：“乃出汉传国玺，投之地以授舜……玺上螭一角微崩。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'She brought out the Han Heirloom Seal and hurled it to the ground for Wang Shun; one horn of the dragon was slightly chipped.'
第 5 站 宜阳（今河南洛阳宜阳）Yiyang (Yiyang, Luoyang)
27年 · 东汉光武帝·建武三年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

宜阳受降·玺归光武：新莽亡后玉玺转入赤眉军；建武三年冯异破赤眉于崤底，刘盆子肉袒降于宜阳，奉玺归光武帝。

Liu Penzi Surrenders the Seal to Guangwu at Yiyang: After Wang Mang’s fall the seal passed to the Red Eyebrows; in 27 CE at Yiyang, Liu Penzi surrendered the Heirloom Seal to Emperor Guangwu.

《资治通鉴》：“盆子及丞相徐宣以下三十余人肉袒降，上所得传国玺绶。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Liu Penzi and Chancellor Xu Xuan bared their shoulders in surrender at Yiyang and presented the Heirloom Seal and ribbon.'
第 6 站 洛阳（今河南洛阳）Luoyang (Luoyang, Henan)
191年 · 东汉献帝·初平二年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

洛阳修陵·孙坚得玺：董卓焚洛阳西迁时中官投玺于井；初平二年孙坚进兵洛阳扫除汉宗庙，于城南甄官井探得传国玺。

Sun Jian Retrieves the Seal from the Palace Well in Luoyang: After Dong Zhuo burned Luoyang, General Sun Jian entered the ruined capital in 191 CE, swept the Han temples, and recovered the seal from Zhenguan Well.

《资治通鉴》：“坚乃扫除宗庙，祠以太牢；得传国玺于城南甄官井中。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Sun Jian swept the ancestral temples, sacrificed with the grand offering, and obtained the Heirloom Seal from the Zhenguan Well.'
第 7 站 寿春（今安徽淮南寿县）Shouchun (Shou County, Anhui)
199年 · 东汉献帝·建安四年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

袁术僭号·徐璆献玺：袁术夺孙坚妻传国玺于寿春僭号仲氏；建安四年袁术败死，广陵太守徐璆得玺献还汉献帝于许都。

Yuan Shu Usurps the Imperial Title; Xu Qiu Returns the Seal: Warlord Yuan Shu seized the seal and declared himself emperor at Shouchun; upon his death in 199 CE, Xu Qiu recovered and returned it to the Han court.

《资治通鉴》：“袁术以得传国玺，遂谋称帝……广陵太守徐璆得其传国玺，诣许献之。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Having obtained the Heirloom Seal, Yuan Shu plotted to declare himself emperor... Xu Qiu recovered the Seal and presented it at Xu.'
第 8 站 繁阳（今河南许昌临颍）Fanyang (Xuchang) (Linying, Xuchang)
220年 · 魏文帝·黄初元年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

繁阳受禅·汉魏易代：汉献帝遣张音持节奉玺绶禅位于魏，曹丕于许昌繁阳升受禅坛受玺即位（265年司马炎受禅归晋）。

Cao Pi Receives the Seal of Abdication at Fanyang: In 220 CE Emperor Xian sent the Heirloom Seal to Cao Pi at the Fanyang Abdication Altar to found Cao Wei (later passing to Western Jin in 265 CE).

《资治通鉴》：“使行御史大夫事张音持节奉玺绶诏册，禅位于魏……升坛受玺绶。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Zhang Yin was sent bearing credentials to present the Seal and edict of abdication to Wei; Cao Pi ascended the altar and received it.'
第 9 站 平阳（今山西临汾）Pingyang (Linfen, Shanxi)
311年 · 西晋怀帝·永嘉五年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

永嘉之乱·玺没平阳：永嘉五年汉赵刘曜、王弥攻陷洛阳，俘晋怀帝及传国六玺北迁平阳；后石勒灭前赵，玺入后赵邺城。

Disaster of Yongjia: The Seal Taken to Pingyang: During the 311 CE Disaster of Yongjia, Han-Zhao forces sacked Luoyang and carried Emperor Huai and the Seal to Pingyang (later passing to Later Zhao).

《资治通鉴》：“曜、弥入自宣阳门……迁帝及惠羊后、传国六玺于平阳。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Liu Yao and Wang Mi entered through Xuanyang Gate and removed the Emperor and the Six Heirloom Seals to Pingyang.'
第 10 站 建康（今江苏南京）Jiankang (Nanjing, Jiangsu)
352年 · 东晋穆帝·永和八年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

邺城秘迎·奉还建康：冉魏困守邺城时，晋太守戴施秘令怀玺送枋头，谢尚遣骑迎归建康，历宋齐梁陈相传。

Returned South from Ye to the Eastern Jin Capital Jiankang: During the 352 CE siege of Ye, Jin magistrate Dai Shi smuggled the Seal out to Fangtou; General Xie Shang escorted it to Jiankang for the Southern Dynasties.

《资治通鉴》：“阴令怀玺送于枋头……尚遣振武将军胡彬等骑三百迎玺，致诸建康。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'Dai Shi secretly ordered him to carry the Seal to Fangtou; Xie Shang sent 300 horsemen to escort the Seal to Jiankang.'
第 11 站 江都（今江苏扬州）Jiangdu (Yangzhou, Jiangsu)
589年 · 隋文帝·开皇九年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

平陈入隋·江都流落：隋灭陈收传国玺入大兴宫；618年江都兵变宇文化及夺玺北还，窦建德复得之，后随隋萧后入东突厥。

Sui Conquest of Chen and Fall at Jiangdu: Taken by Sui when Chen fell in 589 CE, the Seal was seized at Jiangdu in 618 CE, recovered by Dou Jiande, and carried with Empress Xiao into the Eastern Turks.

《资治通鉴》：“历宋、齐、梁、陈，至于隋。隋亡，萧后携以入突厥。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'It passed through Song, Qi, Liang, and Chen down to Sui. When Sui fell, Empress Xiao carried it into the Eastern Turks.'
第 12 站 定襄（今内蒙古呼和浩特和林格尔）Dingxiang (Horinger, Inner Mongolia)
630年 · 唐太宗·贞观四年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

李靖破胡·萧后归唐：贞观四年李靖奇袭定襄、大破东突厥于阴山，隋萧后与杨政道归顺至长安，奉传国玺归于大唐。

Li Jing Defeats the Eastern Turks; Empress Xiao Returns the Seal to Tang: In 630 CE, Tang general Li Jing crushed the Eastern Turks at Dingxiang; former Sui Empress Xiao returned to Chang’an and presented the Seal to Emperor Taizong.

《资治通鉴》：“太宗贞观四年，李靖破突厥，萧后与政道奉之归于唐。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'In the fourth year of Zhenguan, Li Jing defeated the Turks, and Empress Xiao with Yang Zhengdao presented the Seal back to Tang.'
第 13 站 洛阳玄武楼（今河南洛阳）Luoyang (Xuanwu Tower) (Luoyang, Henan)
936年 · 后晋高祖·天福元年 · 在地图定格此节点 →

玄武楼焚·千古绝响：石敬瑭引契丹兵逼洛阳，后唐末帝李从珂携传国宝登洛阳玄武楼自焚，传世千余载之秦玺自此失传。

Lost in the Flames of Xuanwu Tower in Luoyang: As Shi Jingtang and Khitan troops closed on Luoyang in 936 CE, Later Tang’s final emperor Li Congke burned himself with the Seal on Xuanwu Tower, and it vanished.

《资治通鉴》：“唐主聚后妃以下及传国宝、器玩，登玄武楼，纵火自焚……自是传国宝失所在。”Zizhi Tongjian: 'The Tang ruler gathered the empress, consorts, and the Heirloom Seal, ascended Xuanwu Tower, and set himself on fire; thereafter the Seal was lost.'
