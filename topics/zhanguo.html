公元前403年周威烈王册命韩、赵、魏为诸侯（《资治通鉴》开篇），前386年田氏代齐，战国七雄（秦、楚、齐、燕、赵、魏、韩）并立争雄。各国推行变法、修筑长城、兴修水利，百家争鸣；秦经商鞅变法与并巴蜀后崛起，秦王嬴政自前230年至前221年十年间次第攻灭韩、赵、魏、楚、燕、齐，终结列国纷争。

Beginning in 403 BCE when King Weilie of Zhou recognized Han, Zhao, and Wei as feudal lords (the opening year of Sima Guang’s Zizhi Tongjian), the Seven Warring States (Qin, Chu, Qi, Yan, Zhao, Wei, Han) vied for supremacy alongside older states such as Lu, Yue, Ba-Shu, and Zhongshan. Strengthened by Shang Yang’s reforms and the conquest of Ba-Shu, King Zheng of Qin annexed all six rival states between 230 and 221 BCE.
