The Long March (Oct 1934 – Oct 1936 · Strategic March from Jiangxi to Shaanxi-Gansu)

起止年代：1934—1936年 推演节点：共 10 站 红军各部Red Army Forces 国民政府军Nationalist Government Forces

1934年10月，第五次反“围剿”失利后，中央红军主力八万六千余人自赣南瑞金、于都渡贡水西行。经湘江苦战突围后改向黔北，1935年1月于遵义召开会议改组军事指挥；随后在川黔滇边四渡赤水迂回穿插，南下渡金沙江、北上夺大渡河泸定桥，翻越夹金山与红四方面军于懋功会合，再经松潘草地、甘南腊子口于10月抵陕北吴起镇；1936年10月红一、二、四方面军于甘肃会宁及宁夏将台堡会合，历时两载、纵横十余省。

In October 1934, following the Fifth Encirclement Campaign, over 86,000 Central Red Army troops departed Ruijin and Yudu in southern Jiangxi. After a costly crossing of the Xiang River, the column pivoted into Guizhou, where the January 1935 Zunyi Conference reorganized military command; maneuvering across the Chishui, Jinsha, and Dadu rivers, the Red Army scaled Mount Jiajin to meet the Fourth Front Army at Maogong, crossed the Songpan Grasslands and Lazikou Pass to reach northern Shaanxi in October 1935, and converged with the Second and Fourth Front Armies at Huining in October 1936.

全线关键节点：瑞金（今江西瑞金·于都） → 全州（今广西全州·兴安） → 遵义（今贵州遵义） → 习水（今贵州习水·仁怀茅台） → 禄劝（今云南禄劝皎平渡） → 泸定（今四川甘孜泸定） → 懋功（今四川阿坝小金） → 迭部（今甘肃迭部腊子口） → 吴起（今陕西延安吴起） → 会宁（今甘肃会宁·宁夏将台堡）。

史料出处：中共中央党史和文献研究院《中国共产党一百年大事记》(1921年7月—2021年6月) 1934年10月—1936年10月条

红军长征 · 全程 10 幕时空推演、古今地名与史料引文详解

一 · 赣南突围与遵义改向 Act I · Breakout from Jiangxi & Pivot to Zunyi
第 1 幕红军各部 瑞金（今江西瑞金·于都）· 瑞金出发·夜渡于都 Ruijin (Ruijin & Yudu, Jiangxi) — Departure from Ruijin & Night Crossing at Yudu
1934年 · 1934年10月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）、邓小平（Deng Xiaoping）
进军方向：自 南昌（北路军） 推进至 瑞金

第五次反“围剿”失利后，中央红军主力第一、三、五、八、九军团及中央纵队共八万六千余人自瑞金出发，于都集结后架设浮桥连夜南渡贡水西行。

Following the Fifth Encirclement Campaign, over 86,000 Central Red Army troops assembled at Yudu from Ruijin and crossed the Gong River by night on pontoon bridges to march west.

陆定一《长征歌》：“十月里来秋风凉，中央红军远征忙；星夜渡过于都河，古陂新田打胜仗。”Long March Ballad: 'Cool blows the autumn wind in the tenth month as the Central Red Army sets out on its distant march, crossing the Yudu River under a starry night.'
第 2 幕红军各部 全州（今广西全州·兴安）· 抢渡湘江·折损过半 Quanzhou (Xiang River) (Quanzhou, Guangxi) — Crossing the Xiang River
1934年 · 1934年11月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）
进军方向：自 衡阳（湘军） / 桂林（桂军） 推进至 全州

红军连破赣南、粤北、湘南三道封锁线后，遭湘军、桂军及中央军近三十万兵力合围于桂北湘江；苦战五昼夜掩护主力渡江，全军由八万六千余人锐减至三万余人。

After passing three blockade lines, the Red Army was converged upon by nearly 300,000 Hunan, Guangxi, and Central government troops along the Xiang River, holding the crossing corridor for five days while losing over half its force.

桂北民谚：“三年不饮湘江水，十年不食湘江鱼。”Local folk saying: 'For three years drink no water from the Xiang River; for ten years eat no fish from the Xiang.'
第 3 幕红军各部 遵义（今贵州遵义）· 通道改向·遵义会议 Zunyi (Zunyi, Guizhou) — Pivot to Guizhou & the Zunyi Conference
1935年 · 1935年1月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）、邓小平（Deng Xiaoping）

渡过湘江后，红军放弃北上湘西原议，经通道、黎平改向防守薄弱的黔北，强渡乌江攻占遵义；1月中旬召开政治局扩大会议改组军事指挥，增选毛泽东为政治局常委，由阵地防御转为灵活运动战。

Abandoning the heavily guarded route toward western Hunan, the column pivoted into northern Guizhou, crossed the Wu River, and took Zunyi; in mid-January 1935 the enlarged Politburo conference reorganized military command and shifted strategy to mobile warfare.

遵义会议决议纪要：“改变黎平会议以黔北为中心之决定，渡江入川，于运动战中寻机破敌。”Zunyi Conference Resolution: 'Revise the Liping decision centered on northern Guizhou, cross the Yangtze into Sichuan, and seek opportunities in mobile warfare.'
二 · 四渡赤水与万里渡江 Act II · Four Crossings of Chishui & the Southwest Rivers
第 4 幕红军各部 习水（今贵州习水·仁怀茅台）· 四渡赤水·攻克娄山 Xishui (Chishui River) (Xishui & Maotai, Guizhou) — Four Crossings of the Chishui River & Loushan Pass
1935年 · 1935年1月—3月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）
进军方向：自 重庆（川军） / 贵阳（薛岳部） 推进至 习水

川军北堵长江、薛岳部南压黔北之际，红军于川黔滇边四渡赤水往返穿插：二渡回师攻克娄山关、重占遵义，三渡自茅台西进诱川黔各军西聚，旋即四渡赤水折向南下二渡乌江。

With Sichuan forces blocking the Yangtze to the north and Xue Yue's columns pressing from the south, the Red Army crossed the Chishui River four times, retaking Loushan Pass and Zunyi on the second crossing before feinting west and slipping south across the Wu River.

毛泽东《忆秦娥·娄山关》：“雄关漫道真如铁，而今迈步从头越。从头越，苍山如海，残阳如血。”Mao Zedong, Loushan Pass: 'Do not say the strong pass is guarded like iron; today we stride across its summit from the beginning—hills blue as the sea, the setting sun like blood.'
第 5 幕红军各部 禄劝（今云南禄劝皎平渡）· 佯逼贵阳·巧渡金沙 Luquan (Jiaopingdu) (Jiaopingdu, Luquan, Yunnan) — Feint at Guiyang & Crossing the Jinsha River
1935年 · 1935年5月上旬 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）

红军南渡乌江后佯逼贵阳，调出滇军东援，主力乘虚疾进云南、虚晃昆明，5月初北趋禄劝皎平渡，凭七只木船历七天七夜全军渡过金沙江入川。

Feinting at Guiyang to draw Yunnan troops east, the main column marched rapidly west past Kunming and turned north to Jiaopingdu in Luquan, crossing the Jinsha River on seven wooden boats over seven days and nights.

毛泽东《七律·长征》：“五岭逶迤腾细浪，乌蒙磅礴走泥丸。金沙水拍云崖暖，大渡桥横铁索寒。”Mao Zedong, The Long March: 'The Five Ridges wind like gentle ripples; majestic Wumeng rolls past like balls of clay. Warm are the cloud-topped cliffs washed by the Jinsha's waves; cold are the iron chains spanning the Dadu River.'
第 6 幕红军各部 泸定（今四川甘孜泸定）· 彝海结盟·抢夺泸定 Luding Bridge (Luding, Sichuan) — Passing the Yi Liangshan & Seizing Luding Bridge
1935年 · 1935年5月下旬 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）
进军方向：自 成都（川军） 推进至 泸定

刘伯承与彝族首领小叶丹于冕宁彝海结盟借道大凉山；先遣队于石棉安顺场渡过大渡河后，因水急船少，红四团沿西岸昼夜疾行二百四十里，二十二名突击队员攀踏十三根铁索夺下泸定桥。

After Liu Bocheng formed an alliance with Yi chieftain Xiao Yedan to pass through the Liangshan mountains and vanguard troops crossed the Dadu River at Anshunchang, the 4th Regiment marched 240 li in a day and night to seize the thirteen-chain Luding Bridge.

清康熙《御制泸定桥碑记》：“泸河水深流急，两岸崇山峻岭……镕铁为索十有三，长四十余丈，飞阁凌虚，天险遂通。”Kangxi Stele at Luding Bridge: 'Where the Dadu waters run deep and swift between sheer cliffs, thirteen iron chains spanning forty zhang link the precipices across the chasm.'
三 · 雪山草地与陕甘会合 Act III · Snow Mountains, Grasslands & Convergence in the Northwest
第 7 幕红军各部 懋功（今四川阿坝小金）· 翻越夹金·懋功会合 Maogong (Xiaojin) (Xiaojin, Sichuan) — Scaling Mount Jiajin & Meeting at Maogong
1935年 · 1935年6月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）
进军方向：自 泸定（红一方面军） / 川陕（红四方面军） 推进至 懋功

6月中旬，中央红军翻越海拔四千余米的夹金山，于川西懋功（今小金）达维镇与自川陕基地西进的红四方面军会合，随后召开两河口、毛儿盖会议商定北上陕甘路线。

In mid-June 1935, the Central Red Army scaled the 4,000-meter snow-covered Mount Jiajin and met the Fourth Front Army from the Sichuan-Shaanxi base at Maogong, subsequently convening at Lianghekou to plan the northward march into Shaanxi and Gansu.

肖华《过雪山草地》：“雪皑皑，野茫茫，高原寒，炊断粮。”Crossing the Snow Mountains and Grasslands: 'White the snow, vast the wilds; bitter cold on the high plateau, and rations gone.'
第 8 幕红军各部 迭部（今甘肃迭部腊子口）· 穿越草地·夺取腊子口 Diebu (Lazikou Pass) (Lazikou, Diebu, Gansu) — Crossing the Songpan Grasslands & Lazikou Pass
1935年 · 1935年8月—9月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）

8月下旬北上右路军自毛儿盖出发，历七昼夜穿越无人区的松潘水草地；9月中旬夜攀绝壁夺取甘南门户腊子口隘口，出山后于哈达铺从报刊得知陕北刘志丹红军活动消息。

Departing Mao'ergai in late August, the northern column spent seven days crossing the uninhabited Songpan marshes, scaled the cliffs of Lazikou Pass in mid-September to enter southern Gansu, and learned from newspapers at Hadapu of Red forces active in northern Shaanxi.

毛泽东《七律·长征》：“更喜岷山千里雪，三军过后尽开颜。”Mao Zedong, The Long March: 'More joyful still are the thousand li of snow on the Min Mountains; once the army has crossed, every face beams.'
第 9 幕红军各部 吴起（今陕西延安吴起）· 翻越六盘·进抵吴起 Wuqizhen (Wuqi, Shaanxi) — Crossing Mount Liupan & Reaching Wuqizhen
1935年 · 1935年10月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）
进军方向：自 迭部（陕甘支队） / 甘泉（陕北红十五军团） 推进至 吴起

部队经通渭榜罗镇北渡渭水、翻越六盘山，于10月19日抵达陕北吴起镇，与刘志丹、徐海东所部陕北红十五军团会合，中央红军结束历时一年的万里转战。

Crossing the Wei River and scaling Mount Liupan via Bangluozhen, the column reached Wuqizhen in northern Shaanxi on October 19, 1935, and joined the local 15th Corps, concluding the Central Red Army's year-long march.

毛泽东《清平乐·六盘山》：“天高云淡，望断南飞雁。不到长城非好汉，屈指行程二万。”Mao Zedong, Mount Liupan: 'High the sky and pale the clouds, we watch the wild geese vanish south. If we reach not the Great Wall we are no true men, counting twenty thousand li.'
第 10 幕红军各部 会宁（今甘肃会宁·宁夏将台堡）· 三路会合·会宁终局 Huining (Huining, Gansu) — Convergence of the Three Front Armies at Huining
1936年 · 1936年10月 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：毛泽东（Mao Zedong）、邓小平（Deng Xiaoping）
进军方向：自 吴起（红一方面军） / 懋功（红四方面军） / 桑植（红二方面军） 推进至 会宁

贺龙、任弼时率红二、六军团自湘西桑植渡金沙江北上，1936年7月于甘孜与红四方面军会合后联袂北进；10月红一、二、四方面军先后于甘肃会宁及宁夏西吉将台堡会合，长征自此落幕。

After He Long and Ren Bishi led the 2nd and 6th Corps from Sangzhi across the Jinsha River to join the Fourth Front Army at Garzê in July 1936, both columns marched north across the grasslands to converge with the First Front Army at Huining and Jiangtaibao in October 1936.

中革军委十月十日通报：“一、四方面军先头部队已于九日在会宁会合，二方面军正经甘南继进。”Military Commission dispatch, Oct 10, 1936: 'Vanguard units of the First and Fourth Front Armies met at Huining on the ninth; the Second Front Army is advancing through southern Gansu.'
