李白仗剑壮游行迹（701年 — 762年 · 武周长安元年至唐宝应元年）

Li Bai's Grand Travels Across Tang China (701 CE – 762 CE · High Tang Dynasty)

时代跨度：701年 — 762年 · 武周长安元年至唐宝应元年 途经站点：共 12 站 起点 → 终点：碎叶城 → 当涂（宣城郡）

全线途经古今地名：碎叶城（今吉尔吉斯斯坦托克马克） → 绵州昌隆（青莲）（今四川江油青莲镇） → 渝州（今重庆） → 荆门（宜都）（今湖北宜都） → 扬州（广陵）（今江苏扬州） → 安陆（今湖北安陆） → 长安（今陕西西安） → 洛阳（今河南洛阳） → 梁宋（睢阳）（今河南商丘） → 齐鲁（兖州任城）（今山东济宁） → 白帝城（夔州）（今重庆奉节） → 当涂（宣城郡）（今安徽马鞍山·当涂）。

Route Stops: Suyab (Anxi Protectorate) (Tokmok, Kyrgyzstan) → Qinglian, Mianzhou (Jiangyou, Sichuan) → Yuzhou (Chongqing) → Jingmen (Yidu) (Yidu, Hubei) → Yangzhou (Guangling) (Yangzhou, Jiangsu) → Anlu (Anlu, Hubei) → Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) → Luoyang (Luoyang, Henan) → Liang-Song (Suiyang) (Shangqiu, Henan) → Qilu (Rencheng, Yanzhou) (Jining, Shandong) → Baidicheng (Kuizhou) (Fengjie, Chongqing) → Dangtu (Xuancheng) (Dangtu, Ma'anshan, Anhui).

李白仗剑壮游行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（12 站）

第 1 站 碎叶城（今吉尔吉斯斯坦托克马克）· 诞于西域·安西碎叶 Suyab (Anxi Protectorate) (Tokmok, Kyrgyzstan) — Birth at Suyab in the Western Regions
701年（长安元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

长安元年（701年），李白诞于唐安西都护府碎叶城（今楚河流域托克马克附近），幼承丝路胡汉交融之风，五岁随父李客东迁入蜀。

Born in 701 CE at Suyab—one of the Four Garrisons of Anxi in the Chu River valley—Li Bai spent his earliest childhood on the Silk Road frontier before migrating with his family to Sichuan at age five.

范传正《唐左拾遗翰林学士李公新墓碑》：“隋末多难，一房被窜于碎叶，流离散落，隐易姓名。”Fan Chuanzheng, New Tomb Inscription for Li Bai: "Amid the turmoil at the end of Sui, his branch of the clan was exiled to Suyab, living incognito until returning to Shu in the Shenlong era."
第 2 站 绵州昌隆（青莲）（今四川江油青莲镇）· 卜居青莲·大匡山读书 Qinglian, Mianzhou (Jiangyou, Sichuan) — Youth in Qinglian & Studying on Mount Dakuang
705年（神龙元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

随父客居剑南道绵州昌隆县青莲乡（自号“青莲居士”）。少时博学广览、好剑术任侠，隐大匡山读书，后游成都、登峨眉，受道士司马承祯赞为“有仙风道骨，可与神游八极之表”，作《大鹏遇希有鸟赋》。

Settling in Qinglian in Mianzhou (whence his sobriquet "Layman of Blue Lotus"), young Li Bai practiced swordsmanship, studied on Mount Dakuang, climbed Mount Emei, and was praised by Taoist master Sima Chengzhen as having "immortal bones."

李白《上安州裴长史书》：“五岁诵六甲，十岁观百家……十五观奇书，作赋凌相如。”Li Bai, Letter to Administrator Pei of Anzhou: "At five I recited the calendar classics; at ten I read the Hundred Schools; at fifteen I composed rhapsodies rivaling Sima Xiangru."
第 3 站 渝州（今重庆）· 夜发清溪·思君下渝州 Yuzhou (Chongqing) — Sailing Down the Min & Yangtze Rivers to Yuzhou
724年（开元十二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

年二十四的李白自蜀中启程远游，登峨眉山辞别蜀中山水，乘舟自清溪顺平羌江（青衣江）、岷江东下渝州，写下清丽绝伦的《峨眉山月歌》。

At twenty-four, Li Bai set out from Mount Emei and sailed down the Min and Yangtze rivers to Yuzhou (Chongqing), composing the Moon Over Mount Emei.

李白《峨眉山月歌》：“峨眉山月半轮秋，影入平羌江水流。夜发清溪向三峡，思君不见下渝州。”Li Bai, Moon Over Mount Emei: "A half-wheel of autumn moon above Mount Emei casts its reflection into the flowing Pingqiang River; setting out by night from Qingxi toward the Three Gorges, longing for you unseen as I sail down to Yuzhou."
第 4 站 荆门（宜都）（今湖北宜都）· 仗剑去国·辞亲出三峡 Jingmen (Yidu) (Yidu, Hubei) — Leaving Shu with Sword in Hand Through the Three Gorges
725年（开元十三年） · 在地图定位此站 →

怀济世远志“仗剑去国，辞亲远游”，自渝州穿瞿塘、巫峡、西陵三峡，出荆门山初见江汉平原天地壮阔，写下《渡荆门送别》。

Sailing through the Three Gorges past Jingmen Mountain into the vast Jianghan Plain, Li Bai began his grand travels across the Tang Empire.

李白《渡荆门送别》：“山随平野尽，江入大荒流。月下飞天镜，云生结海楼。”Li Bai, Farewell at Jingmen: "Mountains vanish into the level plain; the Great River rolls into the boundless wild. Beneath the moon a celestial mirror flies; rising clouds weave ocean pavilions."
第 5 站 扬州（广陵）（今江苏扬州）· 漫游吴越·散金三十万 Yangzhou (Guangling) (Yangzhou, Jiangsu) — Grand Tour of Wu-Yue & Spending 300,000 Cash in Yangzhou
726年（开元十四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

顺江东下，登庐山望瀑布、游金陵怀古，转赴繁华甲天下的淮南都会扬州与吴越诸郡，豪爽济困，“不逾一年，散金三十余万”，写就《望庐山瀑布》《静夜思》等千古绝唱。

Sailing down the lower Yangtze past Mount Lu and Jinling to prosperous Yangzhou, Li Bai generously aided destitute scholars—giving away over 300,000 coins within a year—and composed Quiet Night Thought.

李白《上安州裴长史书》：“曩昔东游维扬，不逾一年，散金三十余万，有落魄公子，悉皆济之。”Li Bai: "Formerly traveling east to Yangzhou, in less than a year I scattered more than three hundred thousand in gold, coming to the aid of every stranded gentleman."
第 6 站 安陆（今湖北安陆）· 酒隐安陆·登临黄鹤楼 Anlu (Anlu, Hubei) — Ten Years in Anlu & Farewell at Yellow Crane Tower
727年（开元十五年） · 在地图定位此站 →

西归荆楚，赘于安陆故相许圉师孙女家，隐居白兆山桃花岩所谓“酒隐安陆，蹉跎十年”；其间频游江夏与襄阳，结交孟浩然，于黄鹤楼赋诗送其东下广陵。

Marrying into the family of former Chancellor Xu Yushi in Anlu, Li Bai made Mount Baizhao his home base for a decade, befriending Meng Haoran and seeing him off at the Yellow Crane Tower.

李白《黄鹤楼送孟浩然之广陵》：“故人西辞黄鹤楼，烟花三月下扬州。孤帆远影碧空尽，唯见长江天际流。”Li Bai, Seeing Off Meng Haoran at Yellow Crane Tower: "My old friend bids farewell to the west at Yellow Crane Tower, sailing down to Yangzhou in the misty blossoms of March."
第 7 站 长安（今陕西西安）· 奉诏入京·翰林待诏 Chang'an (Xi'an, Shaanxi) — Summoned to Chang'an — Imperial Hanlin Academy
742年（天宝元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

因道士吴筠及玉真公主举荐，唐玄宗降诏征召入京。李白高歌“仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人”西入长安，贺知章读《蜀道难》惊呼“谪仙人”，玄宗降辇步迎、供奉翰林，赋《清平调》三章。

Summoned to Chang’an by Emperor Xuanzong, Li Bai was hailed by He Zhizhang as a "Banished Immortal" upon reading The Hard Roads of Shu, and served in the Imperial Hanlin Academy.

李白《南陵别儿童入京》：“仰天大笑出门去，我辈岂是蓬蒿人！”Li Bai, Parting from My Children at Nanling for the Capital: "Throwing back my head with a great laugh I stride out the gate—how could men like us remain forever amid the mugwort?"
第 8 站 洛阳（今河南洛阳）· 赐金放还·洛阳逢杜甫 Luoyang (Luoyang, Henan) — Leaving Court & Historic Meeting with Du Fu in Luoyang
744年（天宝三载春） · 在地图定位此站 →

因傲岸不羁遭权贵谗毁，玄宗“赐金放还”。李白东出长安至东都洛阳，与年三十三的杜甫初遇定交，中国诗史双子星于此风云际会。

Granted gold to leave court in 744 CE, Li Bai traveled east to Luoyang where he met the younger Du Fu—a historic meeting hailed as the twin peaks of Tang poetry.

杜甫《寄李十二白二十韵》：“乞归优诏许，遇我宿心亲。醉舞梁园夜，行歌泗水春。”Du Fu, Twenty Rhymes Sent to Li Bai: "Your plea to retire was granted by gracious edict; meeting me, our kindred hearts grew close—dancing drunk in Liang Garden by night, singing along the Si River in spring."
第 9 站 梁宋（睢阳）（今河南商丘）· 梁园豪游·偕杜甫高适猎孟诸 Liang-Song (Suiyang) (Shangqiu, Henan) — Roaming Liang-Song with Du Fu & Gao Shi
744年（天宝三载秋） · 在地图定位此站 →

秋与杜甫、高适同游梁宋（汴州开封、宋州商丘），登梁王吹台怀古、纵猎孟诸泽，饮酒赋诗，在睢阳梁园挥毫题壁作《梁园吟》。

In autumn 744 CE, Li Bai, Du Fu, and Gao Shi roamed the Liang-Song plains around Kaifeng and Shangqiu, ascending the ancient Liang terrace and hunting on the Mengzhu marshes.

李白《梁园吟》：“我浮黄河去京阙，挂席欲进波连山。天长水阔厌远涉，访古始及平台间。”Li Bai, Ballad of Liang Garden: "Floating down the Yellow River away from the palace towers, I hoisted sail across mountain-high waves until reaching the ancient terrace of Liang."
第 10 站 齐鲁（兖州任城）（今山东济宁）· 东游齐鲁·梦游天姥 Qilu (Rencheng, Yanzhou) (Jining, Shandong) — Sojourn in Qilu & Dreaming of Mount Tianmu
745年（天宝四载） · 在地图定位此站 →

寄家山东兖州任城，与杜甫携手同游齐鲁，访范十隐居、登泰山徂徕，于紫极宫受道箓；临别东鲁南游越中时赋《梦游天姥吟留别》，喊出“安能摧眉折腰事权贵，使我不得开心颜”之千古强音。

Making his family home in Rencheng (Shandong) and roaming Qilu with Du Fu, Li Bai composed Dreaming of Mount Tianmu: A Farewell Song before heading south.

李白《梦游天姥吟留别》：“安能摧眉折腰事权贵，使我不得开心颜！”Li Bai, Dreaming of Mount Tianmu: "How can I bow my brow and bend my waist to serve the powerful, denying myself a joyful heart and open brow?"
第 11 站 白帝城（夔州）（今重庆奉节）· 长流夜郎·白帝遇赦还江陵 Baidicheng (Kuizhou) (Fengjie, Chongqing) — Exile to Yelang & Imperial Pardon at Baidicheng
759年（乾元二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

安史之乱起，李白怀平胡复国之志入永王李璘幕府，兵败后系浔阳狱，坐长流夜郎（黔中）；乾元二年春溯江行至巫山白帝城，忽逢关内大旱朝廷大赦天下，惊喜放舟东下江陵，写下《早发白帝城》。

Implicated in Prince Lin of Yong’s campaign during the An Lushan Rebellion, Li Bai was banished toward distant Yelang; reaching Baidicheng in spring 759 CE, he received an imperial amnesty and raced downstream to Jiangling.

李白《早发白帝城》：“朝辞白帝彩云间，千里江陵一日还。两岸猿声啼不住，轻舟已过万重山。”Li Bai, Departing Baidicheng at Dawn: "At dawn I leave Baidicheng amid rainbow clouds; a thousand li to Jiangling I return in a single day. While gibbons on both banks cry without cease, my light skiff has already shot past ten thousand mountains."
第 12 站 当涂（宣城郡）（今安徽马鞍山·当涂）· 赋临路歌·终老当涂青山 Dangtu (Xuancheng) (Dangtu, Ma'anshan, Anhui) — Final Song at Dangtu & Resting by Mount Qing
762年（宝应元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

晚年漂泊金陵、宣城、秋浦之间，闻太尉李光弼统军讨史朝义，六十一岁仍请缨从军，行至金陵因病折返，依族叔当涂令李阳冰；宝应元年十一月赋《临路歌》绝笔，病逝于当涂，终葬谢朓曾游之青山。

After attempting at sixty-one to join Li Guangbi’s army against the rebels, an ailing Li Bai stayed with his kinsman Li Yangbing in Dangtu, composing his final Song Before the Road and passing away in late 762 CE.

李白《临路歌》：“大鹏飞兮振八裔，中天摧兮力不济。馀风激兮万世，游扶桑兮挂石袂。”Li Bai, Song Before the Road: "The Great Peng soared and shook the eight horizons; broken in mid-heaven, its strength gave out—yet its lingering wind stirs ten thousand generations."
