Qin's Conquest of the Six States (238–221 BCE · The Wars That Unified China)

起止年代：前238—前221年 推演节点：共 10 站 秦军Qin Army 东方六国The Six Eastern States

秦王政九年（前238）亲政后，以王翦、王贲父子与内史腾为将，自十七年（前230）至二十六年（前221）十年间先后灭韩、赵、魏、楚、燕、齐：先取韩赵，再乘荆轲刺秦之隙破燕都蓟城、水灌大梁灭魏，倾六十万大军灭楚，终由王贲北扫辽东与代地、南下临淄受齐王建之降。秦王政遂称“始皇帝”，废分封、行郡县，结束自春秋以来五百余年的诸侯分立。

After taking personal rule in 238 BCE, King Zheng of Qin sent Wang Jian, his son Wang Ben and Governor Teng against the six eastern states. Between 230 and 221 BCE Han, Zhao, Wei, Chu, Yan and Qi fell in turn: Han and Zhao first, then the Yan capital after Jing Ke's failed assassination, Daliang under floodwater, Chu to an army of 600,000, and finally Liaodong, Dai and Qi to Wang Ben. Zheng named himself First Emperor and replaced fiefs with commanderies, ending five centuries of rival states.

全线关键节点：咸阳（今陕西咸阳） → 邺城（今河北临漳西南） → 新郑（今河南新郑） → 邯郸（今河北邯郸） → 蓟城（今北京西南） → 大梁（今河南开封） → 寿春（今安徽寿县） → 襄平（今辽宁辽阳） → 临淄（今山东淄博临淄） → 咸阳（今陕西咸阳）。

史料出处：司马迁《史记》 卷6 秦始皇本纪第六（九年亲政至二十六年并天下、分三十六郡；“六王毕，四海一”出杜牧《阿房宫赋》。尉缭语见十年条（前237），与首站所系九年不同）；司马迁《史记》 卷73 白起王翦列传第十三（王翦六十万伐楚，坚壁待楚东撤后追击）；司马迁《史记》 卷81 廉颇蔺相如列传第二十一（李牧附传：王翦急击，“虏赵王迁及其将颜聚，遂灭赵”）；司马迁《史记》 卷43 赵世家第十三（幽缪王七年作“赵怱军破，颜聚亡去”，与李牧传颜聚被虏之说不同）；司马光《资治通鉴》 卷6 秦纪一 始皇帝十八年至十九年（作“杀赵葱，颜聚亡”，从赵世家之说；校记称此句原系十九年，移至十八年）；司马迁《史记》 卷86 刺客列传第二十六（荆轲刺秦与易水歌）；司马迁《史记》 卷88 蒙恬列传第二十八（始皇二十六年蒙恬为将攻齐）

秦灭六国 · 全程 10 幕时空推演、古今地名与史料引文详解

一 · 亲政与东出 Act I · Personal Rule & the Turn East
第 1 幕秦军 咸阳（今陕西咸阳）· 亲政平乱·谋定东出 Xianyang (Xianyang, Shaanxi) — Personal Rule & a Plan to Take the Six States
前238年 · 秦王政九年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：嬴政（King Zheng of Qin）、李斯（Li Si）

秦王政年二十二于雍城行冠礼亲政，平嫪毐之乱，次年罢吕不韦；纳李斯、尉缭之谋，以重金离间六国君臣，再以兵锋各个击破。

At twenty-two King Zheng took personal rule, crushed Lao Ai's revolt and dismissed Lü Buwei; on the advice of Li Si and Wei Liao he bribed the courts of the six states to divide them before striking each in turn.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》引尉缭语：“秦王为人，蜂准，长目，挚鸟膺，豺声，少恩而虎狼心……诚使秦王得志于天下，天下皆为虏矣。”Records of the Grand Historian, quoting Wei Liao: 'The King of Qin has a high nose, long eyes, the chest of a bird of prey and the voice of a jackal; he shows little mercy and has the heart of a tiger or wolf... should he have his way with the realm, all under Heaven will be his captives.'
第 2 幕秦军 邺城（今河北临漳西南）· 攻赵取邺·灭国序幕 Ye (Linzhang, Hebei) — Taking Ye from Zhao: The Opening Blow
前236年 · 秦王政十一年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：王翦（Wang Jian）
进军方向：自 咸阳 推进至 邺城

乘赵军北攻燕国之机，王翦、桓齮、杨端和三路伐赵，连拔邺城、阏与等九城，拉开十年灭国之战的序幕。

While Zhao's army was away fighting Yan, Wang Jian, Huan Yi and Yang Duanhe struck Zhao and took Ye and eight other cities, opening ten years of conquest.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》：“十一年，王翦、桓齮、杨端和攻邺，取九城。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'In the eleventh year Wang Jian, Huan Yi and Yang Duanhe attacked Ye and took nine cities.'
二 · 韩赵先亡 Act II · Han and Zhao Fall First
第 3 幕秦军 新郑（今河南新郑）· 内史腾灭韩·六国首灭 Xinzheng (Xinzheng, Henan) — Governor Teng Takes Han, the First State to Fall
前230年 · 秦王政十七年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：内史腾（Governor Teng）
进军方向：自 南阳（假守腾） 推进至 新郑

上年韩献南阳之地，秦以腾为假守；是年内史腾自南阳北上攻韩，俘韩王安，尽纳其地置颍川郡，韩于六国中最先灭亡。

A year after Han ceded Nanyang, Teng marched north from there, captured King An and annexed Han as Yingchuan Commandery: the first of the six states to fall.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》：“十七年，内史腾攻韩，得韩王安，尽纳其地，以其地为郡，命曰颍川。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'In the seventeenth year Governor Teng attacked Han, took King An, annexed all his land and made it a commandery named Yingchuan.'
第 4 幕秦军 邯郸（今河北邯郸）· 王翦克邯郸·破灭赵国 Handan (Handan, Hebei) — Wang Jian Takes Handan, Ending Zhao
前228年 · 秦王政十九年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：王翦（Wang Jian）、李牧（Li Mu）
进军方向：自 井陉（王翦） 推进至 邯郸

王翦下井陉、杨端和出河内两路攻赵，李牧拒守经年；秦以重金买通赵王宠臣郭开，赵王迁冤杀李牧，王翦遂大破赵军、克邯郸俘赵王迁，公子嘉北奔代地自立。

Wang Jian came down through Jingxing while Yang Duanhe advanced from Henei; Li Mu held them off until Qin bribes led King Qian to have him killed. Wang Jian then broke the Zhao army, took Handan and captured the king, while Prince Jia fled north to Dai.

《史记·廉颇蔺相如列传》：“后三月，王翦因急击赵，大破杀赵葱，虏赵王迁及其将颜聚，遂灭赵。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'Three months later Wang Jian pressed his attack on Zhao, crushed it and killed Zhao Cong, captured King Qian and his general Yan Ju, and so ended Zhao.'
三 · 易水、大梁与寿春 Act III · The Yi River, Daliang & Shouchun
第 5 幕秦军 蓟城（今北京西南）· 图穷匕见·易水破燕 Jicheng (Beijing) — The Dagger in the Map & Victory on the Yi
前226年 · 秦王政二十一年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：荆轲（Jing Ke）、王翦（Wang Jian）

前227年燕太子丹遣荆轲献督亢地图刺秦王未果；秦遂命王翦伐燕，于易水之西大破燕代联军，次年攻拔燕都蓟城，燕王喜退保辽东。

After Jing Ke's attempt on the King of Qin in 227 BCE failed, Wang Jian routed the Yan and Dai armies west of the Yi River and took the Yan capital Jicheng; King Xi fell back to Liaodong.

《史记·刺客列传》：“风萧萧兮易水寒，壮士一去兮不复还！”Records of the Grand Historian: 'The wind sighs, the Yi River is cold; the hero, once gone, will not return.'
第 6 幕秦军 大梁（今河南开封）· 王贲水灌大梁·魏国覆亡 Daliang (Kaifeng, Henan) — Wang Ben Floods Daliang, Ending Wei
前225年 · 秦王政二十二年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：王贲（Wang Ben）

王贲攻魏，引黄河、鸿沟之水灌大梁，三月城坏，魏王假出降，秦尽取魏地，三晋至此皆入于秦。

Wang Ben turned the Yellow River and the Honggou Canal onto Daliang; after three months the walls gave way and King Jia surrendered, completing Qin's conquest of the three Jin states.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》：“二十二年，王贲攻魏，引河沟灌大梁，大梁城坏，其王请降，尽取其地。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'In the twenty-second year Wang Ben attacked Wei and turned the river and canal onto Daliang; its walls broke, its king asked to surrender, and all his land was taken.'
第 7 幕秦军 寿春（今安徽寿县）· 王翦六十万灭楚·攻克寿春 Shouchun (Shouxian, Anhui) — Wang Jian's 600,000 Conquer Chu
前223年 · 秦王政二十四年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：王翦（Wang Jian）、项燕（Xiang Yan）
进军方向：自 陈·平舆（王翦六十万） 推进至 寿春

李信、蒙恬二十万伐楚大败，秦王亲赴频阳请王翦复出，倾国六十万南下；王翦坚壁不战、休士养锐，待楚军东撤乃追击，于蕲南大破楚军，项燕兵败身死，遂克寿春俘楚王负刍。

After Li Xin's 200,000 were routed, the king himself recalled Wang Jian, who took 600,000 men south, refused battle until the Chu army withdrew east, then struck: Xiang Yan died in defeat and Shouchun fell with King Fuchu.

《史记·白起王翦列传》：“荆数挑战而秦不出。乃引而东。翦因举兵追之，令壮士击，大破荆军。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'Chu challenged them again and again, but Qin would not come out. Chu then withdrew east; Wang Jian pursued with his whole army, sent his best men in, and routed the Chu army.'
四 · 六王毕，四海一 Act IV · Six Kings Ended, the Four Seas United
第 8 幕秦军 襄平（今辽宁辽阳）· 远征辽东·灭燕平代 Xiangping (Liaoyang, Liaoning) — The Liaodong Campaign: Yan and Dai Fall
前222年 · 秦王政二十五年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：王贲（Wang Ben）

王贲远征辽东，攻破襄平俘燕王喜，回师西击代地俘代王嘉；同年王翦平定楚江南故地，降越君，置会稽郡。

Wang Ben took Xiangping in Liaodong and captured King Xi of Yan, then turned west to capture King Jia of Dai; the same year Wang Jian pacified the old Chu lands south of the Yangtze and set up Kuaiji Commandery.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》：“二十五年，大兴兵，使王贲将，攻燕辽东，得燕王喜。还攻代，虏代王嘉。王翦遂定荆江南地，降越君，置会稽郡。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'In the twenty-fifth year a great army under Wang Ben attacked Liaodong, took King Xi of Yan, then turned on Dai and captured King Jia. Wang Jian pacified Chu's lands south of the Yangtze, received the submission of the lords of Yue, and set up Kuaiji Commandery.'
第 9 幕秦军 临淄（今山东淄博临淄）· 王贲南下·齐王建降 Linzi (Zibo, Shandong) — Wang Ben Strikes South; King Jian of Qi Surrenders
前221年 · 秦王政二十六年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：王贲（Wang Ben）、蒙恬（Meng Tian）
进军方向：自 燕南（王贲） 推进至 临淄

齐王建与相后胜发兵守西界、断绝与秦往来；王贲、蒙恬自燕南下直入临淄，齐王建不战而降，六国至此尽灭。

King Jian of Qi and his minister Hou Sheng guarded the western border and cut ties with Qin, but Wang Ben and Meng Tian came south from Yan straight into Linzi; Jian surrendered without a fight, and the last of the six states was gone.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》：“二十六年，齐王建与其相后胜发兵守其西界，不通秦。秦使将军王贲从燕南攻齐，得齐王建。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'In the twenty-sixth year King Jian of Qi and his minister Hou Sheng sent troops to hold the western border and cut off Qin. Qin sent General Wang Ben south from Yan to attack Qi, and took King Jian.'
第 10 幕秦军 咸阳（今陕西咸阳）· 称始皇帝·郡县天下 Xianyang (Xianyang, Shaanxi) — The First Emperor & the Realm in Commanderies
前221年 · 秦始皇二十六年 · 沙盘推演此站 →
核心人物：秦始皇（Qin Shi Huang）、李斯（Li Si）

秦王政议定帝号，自称“始皇帝”；从李斯之议废分封，分天下为三十六郡，收天下兵器铸金人十二，一法度衡石丈尺，车同轨、书同文字。

King Zheng took the title First Emperor; on Li Si's advice he abolished fiefs and divided the realm into thirty-six commanderies, melted the realm's weapons into twelve bronze statues, and standardised laws, weights, measures, axle widths and script.

《史记·秦始皇本纪》：“分天下以为三十六郡……一法度衡石丈尺。车同轨。书同文字。”Records of the Grand Historian: 'He divided the realm into thirty-six commanderies... made one standard for laws, weights and measures; carts had the same axle width, and writing used the same script.'
