秦灭六国，首次建立大一统的中央集权帝国。推行郡县制、书同文、车同轨，北筑长城，南征百越。

Qin conquered the six rival states to found China’s first centralized empire, standardizing script, coinage, weights, and axles, linking the northern Great Wall, and pushing south into Lingnan.
