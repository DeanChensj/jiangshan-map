Su Shi's Literary Exile Across Song China (1056 CE – 1101 CE · Northern Song Dynasty)

苏轼宦海贬谪行迹 · 逐站时空坐标、古今地名与史料诗词详解（13 站）

第 1 站 眉州（今四川眉山）· 弱冠出蜀·仗剑远游 Meizhou (Meishan, Sichuan) — Leaving Meizhou for the Imperial Capital
1056年（嘉祐元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

年二十一与弟苏辙随父苏洵自眉州启程，出岷江、越秦岭赴汴梁应试。

At twenty-one, Su Shi set out from Meizhou with his father Su Xun and brother Su Zhe for the imperial exams in Kaifeng.

苏轼《初发嘉州》：“朝发鼓阗阗，西风猎画旃。故乡飘已远，往意浩无边。”Su Shi, Departing Jiazhou: "Morning drums thunder as the west wind snaps our painted banner; home drifts far behind, our aspirations boundless."
第 2 站 东京开封府（今河南开封）· 金榜题名·名动京师 Dongjing Kaifeng (Kaifeng, Henan) — Triumph at the Kaifeng Imperial Court
1057年（嘉祐二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

嘉祐二年春闱，欧阳修读其《刑赏忠厚之至论》击节叹赏，兄弟同科及第。

Examiner Ouyang Xiu marveled at Su Shi’s 1057 examination essay as the Su brothers passed together to instant fame.

欧阳修《与梅圣俞书》：“读轼书，不觉汗出，快哉快哉！老夫当避路，放他出一头地也。”Ouyang Xiu: "Reading Su Shi’s essay brought beads of sweat in sheer delight—this old man must step aside and let him stand a head above!"
第 3 站 凤翔府（今陕西凤翔）· 初仕关中·签判凤翔 Fengxiang (Fengxiang, Shaanxi) — First Official Post in Fengxiang
1061年（嘉祐六年） · 在地图定位此站 →

制科入三等，任签书凤翔府判官，兴修东湖、革衙前弊政，作《喜雨亭记》。

Appointed magistrate-assessor in Fengxiang, where he dredged East Lake, reformed corvée, and wrote Pavilion of Joyful Rain.

苏轼《喜雨亭记》：“使天而雨珠，寒者不得以为襦；使天而雨玉，饥者不得以为粟。”Su Shi, Pavilion of Joyful Rain: "Were Heaven to rain pearls, the cold could not wear them as coats; were it to rain jade, the hungry could not eat it as grain."
第 4 站 杭州（今浙江杭州）· 外放通判·寄情西湖 Hangzhou (Hangzhou, Zhejiang) — Vice-Prefect of Hangzhou & West Lake
1071年（熙宁四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

因上书直陈新法之弊自请外放任杭州通判，巡行属县赈灾，赋西湖诗篇。

Opposing Wang Anshi’s New Policies, he served as Vice-Prefect of Hangzhou, relieving famine and immortalizing West Lake in verse.

苏轼《饮湖上初晴后雨》：“欲把西湖比西子，淡妆浓抹总相宜。”Su Shi: "If I may compare West Lake to Lady Xishi, in light makeup or rich adornment she is equally peerless."
第 5 站 密州（今山东诸城）· 知密州·超然豪放 Mizhou (Zhucheng, Shandong) — Governor of Mizhou — Birth of the Heroic Style
1075年（熙宁八年） · 在地图定位此站 →

知密州抗旱捕蝗，于超然台赋《水调歌头》《江城子·密州出猎》，开豪放词风。

As Governor of Mizhou, he pioneered heroic ci-poetry with "When Will the Bright Moon Be" and "Hunting at Mizhou."

苏轼《水调歌头》：“人有悲欢离合，月有阴晴圆缺，此事古难全。但愿人长久，千里共婵娟。”Su Shi, Prelude to Water Melody: "Men have sorrow and joy, parting and reunion; the moon has clouds and clear skies, waxing and waning."
第 6 站 徐州（今江苏徐州）· 知徐州·抗洪筑黄楼 Xuzhou (Xuzhou, Jiangsu) — Governor of Xuzhou — Holding the Yellow River Dykes
1077年（熙宁十年） · 在地图定位此站 →

黄河决口逼城，苏轼布衣草履庐于城上，率军民筑堤死守七十余日，水退筑黄楼。

When Yellow River floods besieged Xuzhou, he camped on the wall for seventy days to hold the dykes, later building the Yellow Tower.

苏轼《答范淳甫》：“吾在徐州，与民同患难，水退而筑黄楼以镇之。”Su Shi, Letter to Fan Chunfu: "In Xuzhou I shared the perils of flood with the people; when the waters receded, we built the Yellow Tower above the eastern gate."
第 7 站 湖州（今浙江湖州）· 移知湖州·乌台诗案 Huzhou (Huzhou, Zhejiang) — Transfer to Huzhou & the Crow Terrace Poetry Trial
1079年（元丰二年） · 在地图定位此站 →

移知湖州上《谢上表》，遭御史台李定等摘句弹劾“讪谤新法”，被逮赴汴京诏狱。

Impeached over his Huzhou Memorial of Thanks for allegedly satirizing the New Policies, he was arrested and jailed in the Censorate ("Crow Terrace Case").

苏轼《狱中寄子由》：“是处青山可埋骨，他年夜雨独伤神。与君世世为兄弟，更结来生未了因。”Su Shi, From Prison to Ziyou: "Anywhere the green hills may bury my bones; in future years you will grieve alone in the night rain."
第 8 站 黄州（今湖北黄冈）· 贬谪黄州·东坡赤壁 Huangzhou (Huanggang, Hubei) — Exile in Huangzhou — Dongpo & the Red Cliffs
1080年（元丰三年） · 在地图定位此站 →

出狱贬黄州团练副使，躬耕东坡，赋《赤壁赋》《念奴娇》、书《寒食帖》。

Banished to Huangzhou, he farmed the Eastern Slope ("Dongpo") and wrote the Red Cliff Odes and Cold Food Observance.

苏轼《定风波》：“竹杖芒鞋轻胜马，谁怕？一蓑烟雨任平生。”Su Shi, Calming the Waves: "Bamboo staff and straw sandals lighter than a steed—who fears? One straw cloak in misty rain for a lifetime."
第 9 站 登州（今山东烟台蓬莱）· 量移北上·登州海市 Dengzhou (Penglai, Yantai, Shandong) — Prefect of Dengzhou & Recalled to the Academy
1085年（元丰八年） · 在地图定位此站 →

量移汝州过庐山赋《题西林壁》；哲宗即位知登州五日观海市，旋召还任翰林学士。

Transferred north past Mount Lu, he served five days as Prefect of Dengzhou witnessing the Penglai sea mirage before his recall as Hanlin Academician.

苏轼《登州海市》：“东方云海空复空，群仙出没空明中。”Su Shi, Mirage at Dengzhou: "Eastern clouds and sea, void upon void; immortals appear and vanish in the luminous sky."
史料出处：脱脱等《宋史》 卷338 列传第九十七 苏轼传
第 10 站 杭州（今浙江杭州）· 再知杭州·浚湖筑苏堤 Hangzhou (Hangzhou, Zhejiang) — Governor of Hangzhou — Building the Su Causeway
1089年（元祐四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

再知杭州疏浚西湖、筑三十里“苏公堤”，设安济坊救疫（后历知颍、扬、定州）。

Returning as Governor of Hangzhou, he dredged West Lake, built the Su Causeway, and founded the Anji public infirmary (later governing Yingzhou, Yangzhou, and Dingzhou).

《宋史·苏轼传》：“轼取葑田积湖中，南北径三十里，为长堤以通行者，吴人种菱，歌之曰‘苏公堤’。”History of Song: "Su Shi piled the cleared lake silt into a thirty-li causeway across West Lake, which the people of Wu gratefully named the Su Causeway."
史料出处：脱脱等《宋史》 卷338 列传第九十七 苏轼传
第 11 站 惠州（今广东惠州）· 再贬岭南·寓居惠州 Huizhou (Huizhou, Guangdong) — Banished South of the Nanling to Huizhou
1094年（绍圣元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

绍圣初哲宗亲政新党复起，年近六旬自定州远贬岭南惠州安置，捐犀带助修东西新桥。

When the New Faction returned to power in 1094, the nearly sixty-year-old Su Shi was banished from Dingzhou across the Nanling to Huizhou.

苏轼《食荔枝》：“日啖荔枝三百颗，不辞长作岭南人。”Su Shi, Eating Lychees: "Feasting on three hundred lychees a day, I would gladly remain a man of Lingnan forever."
第 12 站 儋州（今海南儋州）· 渡海琼州·敷文儋耳 Danzhou (Danzhou, Hainan) — Across the Sea to Danzhou (Hainan Island)
1097年（绍圣四年） · 在地图定位此站 →

六十二岁再贬琼州昌化军（儋州），居桄榔庵讲学授徒，培养出海南首位举人姜唐佐。

Banished at sixty-two across the strait to Danzhou on Hainan Island, he taught in a palm-thatched hut and mentored Hainan’s first graduate.

苏轼《六月二十日夜渡海》：“九死南荒吾不恨，兹游奇绝冠平生。”Su Shi, Crossing the Sea at Night: "Nine deaths in the southern wilds leave me no regret—this wondrous journey crowns my entire life."
第 13 站 常州（今江苏常州）· 遇赦北归·终老毗陵 Changzhou (Changzhou, Jiangsu) — Pardoned Return North & Final Rest in Changzhou
1101年（建中靖国元年） · 在地图定位此站 →

徽宗即位大赦北归，渡海沿赣江北上，建中靖国元年七月病逝于常州，年六十六。

Pardoned upon Emperor Huizong’s accession, he journeyed north by boat and passed away in Changzhou in August 1101 at age sixty-six.

苏轼《自题金山画像》：“心似已灰之木，身如不系之舟。问汝平生功业，黄州惠州儋州。”Su Shi, Inscription on My Portrait at Jinshan: "My heart like ashen wood, my body an unmoored boat. Ask of my life’s achievements: Huangzhou, Huizhou, Danzhou."
